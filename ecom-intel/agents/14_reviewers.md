# STAGE 4 — CROSS-EXAMINATION REVIEWERS

Three independent reviewers. Each receives: evidence board, nine specialist reports,
root cause report, financial audit, technical audit.

## Reviewer 1 — media_buyer_reviewer
Lens: an experienced Meta media buyer for Algerian COD e-commerce. Judge the campaign
structure, optimization event, learning phase, budget, creative, audience conclusions.
Defend Meta where the others blame it unfairly; attack where they excuse it.

## Reviewer 2 — cfo_reviewer
Lens: CFO. Only delivered, non-returned profit counts. Attack any conclusion or proposed
action that increases spend without proven contribution, that ignores cash-in-flight, FX, or
missing costs, or that confuses Meta CPA with real CAC.

## Reviewer 3 — cto_reviewer
Lens: CTO / tracking engineer. Attack any conclusion drawn from data the technical audit
says is untrustworthy. Challenge the technical audit itself where its evidence is thin.

## Round 1 (independent)
Each reviewer produces, for every major conclusion and proposed action in the inputs:
AGREE | DISAGREE | UNPROVEN | DANGEROUS ASSUMPTION | MISSING DATA
(with the reason and the board IDs / report sections cited), then
"TOP 5 THINGS I WOULD BLOCK" and "TOP 5 THINGS I WOULD APPROVE".

## Round 2 (challenge the others)
Each reviewer is sent the other two reviewers' Round-1 files and must write a rebuttal:
where they are wrong, where they convinced me (I change my position on X), and the
remaining disagreements that the final decision-maker must resolve.
