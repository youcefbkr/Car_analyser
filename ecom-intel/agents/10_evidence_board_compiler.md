# STAGE 2 — evidence_board_compiler (orchestrator's instrument)

MISSION: Organize — do NOT re-analyze, do NOT decide, do NOT recommend.
Read all nine Stage-1 reports (paths in your prompt) and build a structured EVIDENCE BOARD.
You are a clerk with a strict template. Where agents disagree, record both claims and the
contradiction; do not resolve it. Where the adversarial agent weakened/refuted a claim,
carry that verdict next to the claim. Carry sample sizes.

Each claim row MUST have: ID | CLAIM | SOURCE (agent + underlying tool) | TIME PERIOD |
CONFIDENCE (H/M/L, after adversarial verdict) | STATUS (Verified / Calculated / Inferred /
Hypothesis) | SAMPLE (n) | ADVERSARIAL VERDICT (Survives/Weakened/Refuted/Not attacked)

Sections (in this order, each a table):
A. FACTS (verified or calculated only)
B. HYPOTHESES
C. CONTRADICTIONS (claim X by agent A vs claim Y by agent B, numbers side by side)
D. ANOMALIES
E. FINANCIAL IMPACT (every money figure, its currency, FX assumption, and whether it is an estimate)
F. TECHNICAL ISSUES
G. MARKETING ISSUES
H. OPERATIONAL ISSUES
I. UNKNOWN VARIABLES (what nobody could measure, and which decision it blocks)
J. KEY NUMBERS SNAPSHOT (single table: spend, impressions, clicks, LPV, Meta purchases,
   Tassyir orders, confirmed, shipped, delivered, returned, in-flight, revenue by stage —
   per window, with the source for each cell; mark conflicting cells)

Keep claim IDs stable (F-01, H-01, C-01, ...) so later agents can cite them.
