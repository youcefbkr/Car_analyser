# Women's Phone Bag: UGC Face-Talk (6-Agent Creative War Room)

Market: Algeria · Women 18–34 · Meta (FB + IG) · UGC face-talk · Algerian Darija
Offer: 2400 DA (possible 2700 DA) · Cash on delivery · Delivery to 58 wilayas

---

## 1. Executive Diagnosis

**Verdict:** The original understands the product but reads like a spoken catalogue listing.
- It runs 95 words (~35 seconds) and lists 11 product attributes.
- The hook takes until about 7 seconds to finish.
- The strongest emotional line sits at the 80% mark.
- The CTA has no price, no COD and no specific action.
- The language keeps sliding from Darija into MSA or "marketing Darija". That sliding is what makes it sound like «publicité».

|---|---|---|

| 2 | What goes | Spoken water resistance · "protects your phone" (said twice) · «الزينة الصغيرة» · «عملية» / «عصري» · «تبدّل كامل اللوك» · «ديري طلبك الآن» · all MSA |

## 2. Agent 1: Performance Creative Strategist

**The first 3 seconds.** By second 3 the viewer has heard «بنات! علاش تبقاي رافدة تلفونك في يدك طول…». The sentence isn't finished, the product isn't visible and nothing shows the problem. The thumb-stop rests on «بنات» alone.

**Attention map (original, at a natural pace of about 2.7 words per second):**

| ~Time | Line | What happens to attention |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7s | Hook question | Finishes too late. Two benefits compete. "Why do you…" puts her on the defensive. |
| 7–13s | «صغيرة، خفيفة وعملية بزاف…» | **Drop #1.** A stack of adjectives and nothing new to see. The hands-free line is good but buried here. |
| 13–21s | Strap, mesh, ornament, «مختلف الإطلالات» | **Drop #2.** A 23-word feature dump. |
| 21–27s | Protection, water, colors | **Drop #3.** The third feature wave. Anyone not sold yet leaves here. |
| 27–31s | «ماشي غير حقيبة للهاتف…» | The best emotional idea arrives after most viewers have gone. |
| 31–35s | CTA | No price, no COD, no reason to act. |

**Benefit hierarchy:**
1. Phone on you, hands free. This is the functional problem, so it belongs in the hook.
2. It's pretty and reads as an accessory. This is the purchase trigger.
3. Light and adjustable. This removes the "annoying to wear" doubt.
4. Colors. Choice feeds the CTA.
5. COD and 58 wilayas. These remove risk.

Cut: water-resistant, "protects", «عملية», «عصري».

**Strongest trigger:** relief plus a little vanity.

**Hook replacements proposed:**
- H1: «بنات، تلفوني يا في يدي… يا ضايع في قاع الصاك!» (two relatable states, visual)
- H2: «بنات، قولولي ماشي غير أنا… تلفوني ديما في يدي!» (confession)
- H3: «هادي أحسن حاجة شريتها هاد العام.» (testimonial hook)
- H4: «تلفونك في يدك في الترامواي؟ غلطة.» (fear/security hook)

**Other recommendations:**
- Put the product in her hand by second 5.
- Add on-screen text from frame 1 for viewers watching without sound.
- Replace «ديري طلبك الآن» with a specific action.
- Cap the video at 20 seconds because it is an impulse product.
- Drop «بنات». Every ad opens with it, so start directly on the problem.

## 3. Agent 2: Algerian Women / Consumer Psychology

**Would she actually say this?**

| Original | Verdict | What she'd actually say |
|---|---|---|
| «بنات!» | ✅ Native | Keep |
| «علاش تبقاي رافدة تلفونك في يدك طول الوقت» | «رافدة» ✅, but «علاش تبقاي…» lectures her | Confess instead: «تلفوني…» |
| «حقيبة الهاتف» | ❌ Nobody says this | «صاك تاع تلفون», or just «هادي» |
| «تلفونك» (L1) → «الهاتف» (L2) | ❌ The register switch itself says "this was written" | Stay with «تلفون» |
| «قابل للتعديل» | ❌ Catalogue MSA | «تطوليه ولا تقصريه» |
| «تصميمها الشبكي» | ❌ | Don't name it, show it |
| «شكل بناتي وعصري» | «بناتي» ✅, «عصري» sounds like a catalogue | «شابة» |
| «مختلف الإطلالات» | ❌ Pan-Arab influencer MSA | «تجي مع اللبسة» |
| «متوفرة بعدة ألوان» | ❌ | «كاينة بكذا لون» |
| «ديري طلبك الآن» | ❌ «الآن» | «اضغطي تحت» / «درك» |
| «التوصيل متوفر لجميع الولايات» | ❌ | «التوصيل لـ58 ولاية» |

**Language nuance.** «حقيبة» probably crept in because the writer needed a feminine noun, and «صاك» is masculine in Darija. Algerian women already solve this: they call it «هادي» ("this thing") and keep the feminine agreement (فيها، شابة، خفيفة). Use «صاك» only as a generic noun, as in «ماشي غير صاك تاع تلفون».

**Benefits that matter to her:**
- It's pretty. This is the main trigger for a 2400 DA impulse buy.
- Her hands are free when she's out: shopping, coffee, university, the tram, visits.
- She finds her phone without digging for it.
- She can match the color to her hijab or outfit.

**Benefits that matter less:**
- "Water-resistant": she can see it's mesh and won't believe it.
- «عملية» and «عصري» say nothing she can picture.

**Her objections:**
1. "Will my phone fit?" Big Samsung, Redmi and iPhone models are common. Saying «صغيرة» makes this doubt stronger.
2. "Does it look like this in real life? Is the material cheap?"
3. "2400 for a little pouch?" and "How much is delivery?"
4. "Is it for teenagers?" «بناتي» can read as childish at 28–34. «شابة» works at every age.
5. "With mesh, will people see my phone?" (minor)

**What makes her think "I need this":** seeing a woman like her with both hands free who looks put-together. She has to see it, not be told it.

**What makes her scroll:** the moment she hears «حقيبة الهاتف هادي…», which sounds like a TV shopping channel.

**What feels too salesy:** «اللمسة الصغيرة اللي تبدّل كامل اللوك تاعك!» and «ديري طلبك الآن».

**Cultural nuances:**
- **Prices in centimes.** Many people say 2400 DA as «ميتين وربعين ألف» and 2700 DA as «ميتين وسبعين ألف». If the price is ever spoken, let the creator say it her way, and always show «2400 دج» on screen so it can't be misunderstood.
- **Hijab.** A large part of the audience wears hijab or jilbab. Show the crossbody over a modest outfit too.
- **Dialect.** Use widely understood central Darija (ديما، بزاف، تاع، كي، درك، شابة). Avoid strongly regional words.
- **French loanwords.** Keep them light and natural (ديركت، اللوك، أكسسوار). More than that sounds try-hard.
- **Timing.** Late September and October is the university rentrée, which matters to the 18–24 segment.

**A2 recommendations:**
- List several scenarios so every woman sees herself: «فالترامواي، فالجامعة، فالسوق، فالعرس».
- Add youth slang, for example «تهبل».
- Keep water resistance, because the rainy season is coming in the north.

## 4. Agent 3: Direct Response Copywriter

**Structure audit:**

| Stage | In the original | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | L1, merged with 2 benefits | A question, not a moment |
| Problem | Implied in L1 | Never shown |
| Desire | L5 (80% mark) | Too late, too abstract |
| Benefits | L2–L4 | 11 attributes: small, light, practical, hands-free, adjustable, mesh, ornament, girly, modern, protects, water-resistant, plus colors |
| Proof / trust | — | Missing |
| Offer | — | No price, no COD |
| CTA | L6 | Generic |

**Redundancy:**
- "Look/style" is said 4 times: «تكمّلي بيه اللوك» / «يجي مع مختلف الإطلالات» / «يجي مع ستايلك» / «تبدّل كامل اللوك».
- "Protect" is said twice: «تحميه» / «تحمي تلفونك».
- Filler: «تقدري» twice, «في نفس الوقت», «يعني».

**Weak words:** عملية، عصري، مختلف، اللمسة الصغيرة، متوفرة، الآن.

**Stronger ways to show the value:**
- Both hands busy while the phone rides on her body.
- Her own phone sliding in, which proves it fits.
- The strap going from short to long.
- A fan of colors.
- The same bag on two outfits.

**Proposed structure, before writing the final:**
1. **HOOK** (0–3s): one concrete problem moment, said as a confession.
2. **RELATE** (3–5s): "It's not just me, right?"
3. **REVEAL + MECHANISM** (5–12s): phone goes in, bag goes on, hands go up, phone comes out fast.
4. **DESIRE** (12–17s): "Look how pretty it is… it's not just a phone bag."
5. **SUPPORT** (17–21s): light, adjustable, colors, all shown quickly.
6. **TRUST / OFFER** (21–24s): COD and 58 wilayas, with the price on screen.
7. **CTA** (24–27s): pick your color, then tap.

**A3 recommendations:**
- Say the price with "only": «وبـ2400 دج برك».
- Prove water resistance with a splash test on camera.
- Use a listicle format, «3 أسباب علاش…», to make the structure clear.

## 5. Agent 4: Performance Critic

1. **A1's H3 is rejected.** «أحسن حاجة شريتها هاد العام» is a generic superlative. If the creator didn't buy it, it's a fabricated testimonial, and it delays the product idea.
2. **A1's H4 (tram/theft) is rejected.** A mesh pouch isn't anti-theft, so the claim is unsupported. Fear is also the wrong emotion for a cute accessory. It attracts people looking for a security product, who then refuse the parcel.
3. **A1's 20-second cap is rejected as arbitrary.** The ad has to carry a hook, fit proof, the hands-free demo, desire, COD/58 and the CTA. At 20 seconds, either desire or trust gets cut. Cut features, not seconds.
4. **Dropping «بنات» is rejected.** It costs half a second and is how Algerian women naturally address each other.
5. **A2's list of scenarios is rejected.** Tram, university, market and wedding is the feature dump again in another form: four scenes described in words, with nothing to see. One scene shown beats four scenes listed.
6. **Scripted slang («تهبل») is rejected.** Forced slang sounds as fake as MSA and skews very young. It's fine only if the creator would say it anyway.
7. **Water resistance is rejected** (see Agent 6).
8. **A3's «برك» is rejected.** "Only" without a reference price is a salesy judgment. It breaks at 2700 and locks the price into the audio.
9. **The splash test is rejected.** Mesh has openings. If water reaches the phone, the demo destroys trust. It would also be demonstrating a claim nobody has verified.
10. **The listicle is rejected.** It turns a face-talk into a presentation.
11. **An assumption to challenge.** "My phone is always in my hand" isn't automatically a pain; plenty of women like holding their phone. The pain is real in specific moments: hands full, or digging through a handbag. So the hook should be a moment, not a habit. That favors H1 over H2.
12. **A contradiction, resolved.** A1 says hands-free is the top benefit; A2 says looks are. Don't pick one, put them in order: **the problem stops the scroll, the beauty sells.**
13. **Do not change:** «بنات»; «تحطي فيها تلفونك وتخلي يديك مرتاحين وين ما تروحي»; «اختاري اللون تاعك»; the "not just a phone bag" idea.
14. **A risk to watch.** H1 has two halves. Said flatly, it lands slower than H2. It needs physical acting (phone up, then digging in the handbag) plus on-screen text. Film H2 as a backup hook.

## 6. Agent 5: UGC Authenticity Critic

**Saying the original out loud:**
- L1 is 19 words. Nobody can say it in one breath, so she'll rush or stumble.
- «حقيبة الهاتف هادي» forces a switch in register that you can hear.
- Nobody tells a friend «وتصميمها الشبكي مع الزينة الصغيرة يعطيها شكل بناتي وعصري يجي مع مختلف الإطلالات».
- «ديري طلبك الآن» is exactly where it turns into «publicité».

**Rules for the final script:**
- Keep lines to about 10 words or fewer: one line, one breath, one action.
- On every benefit line her hands are doing something: phone in, hands up, strap, colors.
- When the words only describe what's already visible (outfits, strap, colors), switch to B-roll and let her voice continue over it.
- Anything that can't be shown on camera leaves the voice: water resistance, "protects", «عملية», «عصري».
- Build in reaction beats:
  - «ماشي غير أنا، صح؟»: eyebrow up, smile.
  - «قداش راهي شابة!»: she looks down at the bag on herself, not at the lens.
  - The CTA: she points down.

**On the other agents:**
- **A1: accept «لقيت» over «شريتها».** «شوفو واش لقيت» is true, because she's holding it, and still personal. Don't let her imply she's used it for months unless that's true.
- **A2: let her pick the slang.** She can swap one or two words into her own dialect (Oran, Constantine, Algiers). Don't script slang for her.
- **A3: accept the structure.** The "proof" slot is filled by what she demonstrates, not by what she says.
- **Shorten the CTA.** «اضغطي على الزر اللي تحت» is too long. «اضغطي تحت», plus pointing and an on-screen arrow, is enough.
- **Film in beats, not lines to memorize.** Do two or three takes per line and keep the small breaths and laughs; don't cut every pause.

## 7. Agent 6: Conversion & Offer Critic

1. **Is the value clear?** It has to be understood by about second 10: phone goes in, hands are free, it looks cute. The original gets there around second 11, but diluted.
2. **How to frame 2400 DA.** The original never shows the price. In Algeria a hidden price fills the comments with «بشحال؟» and makes people bounce off the landing page.
   - Put the price on screen at the offer beat, and in the primary text and headline.
   - Don't use «برك» or «غير» ("only").
   - No crossed-out price and no "promo": none was provided.
3. **If the price becomes 2700 DA.**
   - The script doesn't depend on the price: it only appears in overlays, so you re-export the video instead of reshooting it.
   - Showing desire first matters more at 2700. It crosses the 2500 DA mental threshold, and since the delivery fee isn't specified, the total she hears on the confirmation call could be close to 3000 or more.
   - Test 2700 only on the creative that already wins at 2400.
   - Judge it on delivered orders and confirmation rate, not on submitted orders.
   - Record optional spoken price lines at both prices, so you can test a spoken price later without reshooting.
4. **COD.** Say it out loud, not only on screen. It's the main reason someone trusts an unknown page in Algeria. Say it in Darija («تخلصي غير كي توصلك»), not «الدفع عند الاستلام».
5. **58 wilayas.** Say it and show it. "58" is more concrete than «جميع الولايات», and it tells people in the newer southern wilayas that they're included. Don't say "free", "to your door" or a delivery time; none of that was provided.
6. **CTA.** Name the action and the choice: pick your color, then tap.
7. **A reason to act now.** None was provided, so don't invent one. Replace urgency with ease: nothing to pay upfront, and choose your color now.
8. **The missing trust element.** There are no reviews in the brief, so don't invent any. Build trust honestly instead:
   - The real product in natural light.
   - A close-up of the material.
   - Her real phone going in.
   - Actually wearing it.

   Add real customer feedback in a later version, only once it exists.
9. **Desire before the ask.** The CTA must come after the «شابة / أكسسوار» beat, never before it.
10. **Protecting order quality:**
    - Show only colors that are in stock. An out-of-stock color in the ad means cancelled orders.
    - Check the largest phone that fits before putting any "fits big phones" text on screen.
    - A1's theft angle and A2's water resistance would attract buyers expecting something the product doesn't promise, and they refuse at the door. Both are rejected.
11. **Pushing back on Agent 5.** Keep COD and 58 wilayas as one spoken line. Many Reels viewers listen and don't read the overlays.

## 9. Final Strategic Angle

**Core angle:** Your phone finally has its own place: on you, with both hands free, and it looks like a cute accessory rather than a utility pouch.

**Why this angle wins:**
- **Fashion only** ("it completes your look") creates strong desire, but in a face-talk it barely stops the scroll and is easy to ignore. It gets its own proper test as Version B.
- **Utility only** (hands-free) stops the scroll but creates no desire, and people who buy for utility compare prices.
- **Protection, water resistance or security** are either unsupported or contradicted by the mesh.
- **Price or value** has nothing honest to lean on: no reference price and no discount.

The chosen angle uses both of the product's intended roles, in the order that converts: **utility stops the scroll, beauty closes the sale.**

## 12. Estimated Duration + Word Count

- **Word count:** 66 words (the original has 95).
- **Speaking time:** about 24–25 seconds at a natural on-camera Darija pace (~2.7 words per second).
- **Estimated final length:** about **27 seconds** (range 25–30), including roughly 3 seconds of action: the reveal, taking the phone out, the strap, and the end card.

**Why 27 seconds is right:**
- The ad has four jobs:
  - Stop the scroll: about 3 seconds.
  - Prove how it works (phone in, hands free, phone out): about 7 seconds.
  - Create desire: about 5 seconds.
  - Remove the risk and ask for the order: about 6 seconds.
- Each job gets one line and one visual.
- Any shorter and you lose either the desire or COD/58, and both directly drive orders in a COD market.
- Any longer only brings back features that don't sell: water resistance, "protects", "practical".
- At 2400 DA this is an impulse decision. She doesn't need to be educated; she needs to see it, want it and feel safe ordering.

### Version B: Performance version (a genuinely different angle)

**Why a second version:** Version A opens on a problem. It relies on women who recognize the "phone in my hand / lost in my bag" moment. Version B opens on **desire and choice**:
- **Audience:** it reaches women who don't feel that problem but buy because it's pretty.
- **Hook:** she asks for help («عاونوني نختار») instead of stating a problem.
- **First frame:** colors, not frustration.
- **Commitment:** the viewer starts choosing a color in the very first second.

It's a different concept, so test it against A as a separate ad, not as a variation of A.

## 14. Final CTA

**Spoken (Version A):**
> «تخلصي غير كي توصلك، والتوصيل لـ58 ولاية.
> اختاري اللون تاعك، واضغطي تحت!»

**Why it works:**
- **Order of information:** first no risk (she pays on delivery), then reach (58 wilayas), then the action. The last thing she hears is what to do.
- **"Pick your color":** it makes her picture owning a specific color, which is stronger than "order".
- **No store-checkout words:** no «الآن», no «ديري طلبك».

**On screen during the CTA:** «💵 2400 دج • الدفع عند الاستلام • 🚚 التوصيل لـ 58 ولاية» + «👇 اختاري لونك واطلبي»

«الدفع عند الاستلام» is fine as written text, because people recognize it at a glance. Out loud, stay in Darija.
